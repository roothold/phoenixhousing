Our mission

Housing that restores dignity, not just shelter.

Many veterans return home carrying more than they can say. Phoenix Housing Group exists to give them a safe, well-kept place to live and a program that helps them rebuild routine, purpose and connection.

We believe recovery and stability are built gradually. That is why our program is organized into five distinct stages, each tailored to the individual's level of need, with support that steps down as independence grows.

Learn about who we are
The Phoenix approach

Support that grows lighter as you grow stronger.

Every resident begins at the stage that fits their situation. As they progress, the level of provided services is reduced by 20% per stage, creating a gradual, confidence-building transition toward independent living.

01

Stabilize

Safe housing, daily structure and comprehensive support from day one.

Full support
02

Rebuild

Personal goals, routines and connections to benefits and services take shape.

80% support
03

Grow

Greater responsibility for daily life, with guidance still close at hand.

60% support
04

Transition

Preparation for independent housing, work and community life.

40% support
05

Thrive

Independent living with a light touch of ongoing connection and encouragement.

20% support

What we provide

More than a place to stay.

Our homes are clean, comfortable and thoughtfully maintained. Our program surrounds them with the structure and encouragement that make lasting change possible.

Quality housing

Well-kept residences designed to feel like home, with the stability veterans need to focus on what comes next.

A staged program

Five clearly defined stages that reduce support gradually and celebrate progress along the way.

Personalized support

Services matched to each resident's level of acuity, with flexibility to adjust as personal needs change.

Guidance and structure

Daily routines, accountability and goal setting that rebuild confidence and purpose.

Community

Living alongside fellow veterans who understand the journey and are walking it too.

A path to independence

Every stage moves residents closer to independent living and a life they are proud of.
