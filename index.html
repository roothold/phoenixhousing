The Phoenix Program

Where bureaucracy fails, we excel.

Traditional systems hide behind red tape and waitlists. We provide immediate, frictionless execution and real life change.

Get them clean, before getting them seen

We protect our residents' peace first: breathing room to stabilize mentally, physically and emotionally behind secure doors, before facing the public eye or administrative hoops.

Total transformation over mere housing

We are not just providing beds. We are building sanctuaries of healing and permanent life change, anchored at our North Las Vegas facility and serving all of Southern Nevada.

Frictionless clinical and care access

Direct pipelines to Clark County clinical care, psychiatric support and substance use disorder resources, working with regional health centers, behavioral networks and mobile health teams so care comes to the resident.

Transportation that never stalls progress

Integrated transit planning across RTC of Southern Nevada routes, OnDemand microtransit and our own coordination, so getting between North Las Vegas, Las Vegas and Henderson is never the excuse.

The human heart of the program

Led by those who walked the path.

Our teams are not cold bureaucrats behind desks. They are Certified Peer Recovery Support Specialists: trained professionals with lived experience who know the road because they have traveled it.

  • Frontline stability and crisis de-escalation, day and night
  • Daily mentorship from someone who has been where you are
  • Real trust from day one, not a case number
  • A bridge to clinical care, benefits and community resources

How the program works

Support that grows lighter as you grow stronger.

Every resident begins at the stage that fits their situation. As they progress, the level of provided services is reduced by 20% per stage, creating a gradual, confidence-building transition toward independent living.

01

Stabilize

Safe housing, daily structure and comprehensive support from day one.

Full support
02

Rebuild

Personal goals, routines and connections to care, benefits and services take shape.

80% support
03

Grow

Greater responsibility for daily life, with guidance still close at hand.

60% support
04

Transition

Preparation for independent housing, work and community life.

40% support
05

Thrive

Independent living with a light touch of ongoing connection and encouragement.

20% support

Community impact

Why everyone wins.

Every person we help restore creates a ripple across the valley: for families, neighborhoods, first responders and the public purse.

Restoring lives and families

People across the valley break free from instability, secure employment and reconnect with what matters most.

Safer, stronger neighborhoods

Proactive, structured environments help reduce crime and disorder across urban and suburban corridors.

Saving lives from overdose

A secure, substance-free safety net offers an immediate lifeline and eases the burden on local emergency responders.

Economic relief for the municipality

Every successful transition reduces strain on emergency services, healthcare systems and public resources, saving taxpayers money.

A blueprint for the nation

Anchored in North Las Vegas, this is a scalable, gold-standard model designed to be replicated neighborhood by neighborhood.

Real-world, measurable results

We track outcomes that matter: stable housing, employment, sobriety milestones and reconnected families.
