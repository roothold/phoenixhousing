Housing

A sanctuary, not just a bed.

Our North Las Vegas facility is a clean, secure and fully prepared place to stabilize and rebuild, paired with the structure and support of The Phoenix Program.

Our homes

Clean, secure and cared for.

We hold our residences to a high standard because we believe the quality of a home shapes the quality of a recovery. Warm, calm and never clinical.

Welcoming living spaces

Shared areas that encourage connection and community among residents.

Fully equipped kitchens

Everything needed to cook, plan meals and build healthy daily routines.

Quiet, private rooms

Personal space to rest, reflect and focus on the next step.

What's included

A home, and everything around it.

  • Secure, furnished residences maintained to a high standard
  • Placement in the program stage that matches individual needs
  • Peer recovery specialists on site, day and night
  • Direct pipelines to clinical, psychiatric and recovery care
  • Transit coordination across North Las Vegas, Las Vegas and Henderson
  • A clear plan toward independent living
