Residents, families and partner agencies can request an intake call. Tell us when you are free and we will confirm the time with you.

What to expect

Confidential. Judgment-free. Practical.

Your call is with an intake coordinator or a peer recovery specialist, not an automated line. We will listen, answer questions and, if it is a fit, map out a placement date.

  • About 20 minutes, by phone or text-first if you prefer
  • No paperwork required before the call
  • Families and agencies welcome to call on someone's behalf
  • Confirmation sent before we reach out
