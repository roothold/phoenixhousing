About us

Rooted in Nevada. Committed to those who served.

Phoenix Housing Group was created with one purpose: to give veterans a real foundation for the next chapter of their lives.

Our story

Why the phoenix?

The phoenix is a symbol of renewal, rising stronger from what came before. It is how we see every veteran who walks through our doors.

Too many veterans in Nevada face housing instability after their service ends. We believe the answer is not just a roof, but a structured, supportive path that respects each person's pace and honors their strength. That belief shaped our five-stage program and it shapes how we run every home.

We are a Nevada-based organization and our focus is local: veterans in our communities, housed and supported here at home.

What we stand for

Our values

Dignity

Every resident is treated with respect and given a home worth being proud of.

Progress

We measure success by forward movement, however gradual, toward independence.

Care

Support is personal, flexible and shaped around what each individual needs.

Community

Veterans supporting veterans, and a wider network of partners standing with them.

Accountability

Clear expectations, honest conversations and follow-through on both sides.

Renewal

We believe in second chapters, and we build the conditions for them to begin.
