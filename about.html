About us

Restoration, revitalization and real-world impact.

Phoenix Housing Group exists for the people traditional systems leave waiting. We move when others stall.

Our story

Why the phoenix?

The phoenix rises stronger from what came before. It is how we see every person who walks through our doors, whatever brought them here.

Too many people across the Las Vegas valley are stuck between crisis and help: veterans coming home, people fighting addiction or mental illness, families with nowhere safe to land. Bureaucracy answers them with forms and waitlists. We answer with a key, a real team and a plan.

Our flagship facility in North Las Vegas serves individuals from across Las Vegas, Henderson and Southern Nevada. It is designed as a sanctuary: secure doors, warm light, and people who have walked the path themselves.

The Phoenix Program is powered by Morton Property Group, LLC.

What we stand for

Our operating principles

Peace first

Get them clean, before getting them seen. Stability behind secure doors comes before public exposure or paperwork.

Execution over excuses

Where bureaucracy fails, we excel. No waitlists, no red tape, no judgment.

Care that comes to you

Clinical, psychiatric and recovery support routed directly to the resident.

Lived experience leads

Certified Peer Recovery Support Specialists on the front line, not behind a desk.

Logistics never stall progress

Transit and coordination across the whole valley, so getting there is never the barrier.

Total transformation

Not beds. Sanctuaries of healing and permanent life change.

Community impact

Why everyone wins.

Every person we help restore creates a ripple across the valley: for families, neighborhoods, first responders and the public purse.

Restoring lives and families

People across the valley break free from instability, secure employment and reconnect with what matters most.

Safer, stronger neighborhoods

Proactive, structured environments help reduce crime and disorder across urban and suburban corridors.

Saving lives from overdose

A secure, substance-free safety net offers an immediate lifeline and eases the burden on local emergency responders.

Economic relief for the municipality

Every successful transition reduces strain on emergency services, healthcare systems and public resources, saving taxpayers money.

A blueprint for the nation

Anchored in North Las Vegas, this is a scalable, gold-standard model designed to be replicated neighborhood by neighborhood.

Real-world, measurable results

We track outcomes that matter: stable housing, employment, sobriety milestones and reconnected families.
