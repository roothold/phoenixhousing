Why stages matter

Change sticks when it happens gradually.

Moving straight from crisis to full independence rarely works. Our program is organized into five distinct stages, each tailored to the individual's level of acuity. As participants progress, the amount of provided services and support is reduced by 20% per stage, allowing a gradual transition toward independence while maintaining flexibility based on personal needs.

  • An individual assessment determines the starting stage
  • Support decreases 20% at each stage as capability increases
  • Flexibility to adjust support up or down when life changes
  • Clear milestones so residents can see their own progress
The five stages

What each stage looks like

01

Stabilize Full support

Get clean, before getting seen. Residents settle into safe, secure housing and a predictable daily structure. The focus is on rest, safety and getting connected to clinical care, benefits and services.

02

Rebuild 80% support

With stability in place, residents set personal goals and begin building routines around health, recovery, finances, relationships and purpose, with a peer specialist beside them.

03

Grow 60% support

Residents take on more responsibility for daily life: their schedule, household duties and commitments in the community. Guidance shifts from directing to coaching.

04

Transition 40% support

Preparation for independent living becomes the priority: employment or income stability, budgeting, housing plans and the support network that carries forward.

05

Thrive 20% support

Residents live independently with a light touch of ongoing connection. Check-ins and encouragement continue, so no one makes the final step alone.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is the program for?

Veterans, people facing addiction or mental health challenges, the underhoused, and anyone who feels lost and is ready for a fresh start. You do not need to be a veteran. You do not need to have everything figured out. Come as you are.

Is there a waitlist?

No. Raising your hand takes about three minutes, and an intake coordinator connects with you the same day to talk through options and map out a placement date.

Where is the facility?

Our flagship facility is in North Las Vegas and serves individuals from across Las Vegas, Henderson and the greater valley. Transportation is coordinated so logistics never stall your progress.

Does everyone start at Stage 1?

No. Each participant is assessed individually and placed at the stage that matches their current level of need. Some residents begin further along the path.

What happens if someone needs more support again?

Support can be adjusted up as well as down. A difficult season does not mean starting over.

Can a family member, case manager or agency make a referral?

Yes. Families, case managers, agencies and community organizations are welcome to reach out on someone's behalf through the intake form or by requesting a confidential call.

Ready to take the first step?

Three minutes to raise your hand. A real person follows up the same day.
