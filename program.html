Our program

Five stages. One steady path forward.

Our program consists of five distinct stages, each tailored to the individual's level of acuity. As participants progress, the amount of provided services and support is reduced by 20% per stage, allowing a gradual transition toward independence while maintaining flexibility based on personal needs.

Why stages matter

Change sticks when it happens gradually.

Moving straight from crisis to full independence rarely works. Our stage model gives each resident time to build the habits, relationships and confidence that make independence sustainable.

Placement is based on an individual assessment, so residents begin at the stage that fits their current needs rather than starting from zero. Progress is reviewed regularly and the pace is always set with the resident, not for them.

  • Individual assessment determines the starting stage
  • Support decreases 20% at each stage as capability increases
  • Flexibility to adjust support up or down when life changes
  • Clear milestones so residents can see their own progress
The five stages

What each stage looks like

01

Stabilize Full support

Residents settle into safe, comfortable housing and a predictable daily structure. The focus is on rest, safety and getting connected to the services and benefits each person is entitled to.

02

Rebuild 80% support

With stability in place, residents set personal goals and begin building routines around health, finances, relationships and purpose. Support remains close, but residents begin taking ownership of their plan.

03

Grow 60% support

Residents take on more responsibility for daily life, including managing their schedule, household duties and commitments in the community. Guidance shifts from directing to coaching.

04

Transition 40% support

Preparation for independent living becomes the priority: employment or income stability, budgeting, housing plans and the support network that will carry forward after the program.

05

Thrive 20% support

Residents live independently with a light touch of ongoing connection. Check-ins and encouragement continue, so no one has to make the final step alone.

Built around the person

Principles that guide every stage

Safety first

A secure, respectful home environment is the foundation everything else is built on.

Structure with flexibility

Clear expectations and routines, adjusted thoughtfully when personal needs change.

Progress, not perfection

Setbacks are part of growth. We respond with support, not judgment.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is eligible for the program?

Our program is designed for veterans in Nevada who are seeking stable, supportive housing and are willing to participate in a structured program. Eligibility is determined through an individual conversation and assessment. The easiest way to find out is to submit a housing inquiry.

Does everyone start at Stage 1?

No. Each participant is assessed individually and placed at the stage that matches their current level of need. Some residents begin further along the path.

How long does each stage last?

There is no fixed timeline. Progression is based on readiness and personal goals, reviewed regularly with the resident.

What happens if someone needs more support again?

Support can be adjusted up as well as down. The program maintains flexibility so that a difficult season does not mean starting over.

Can family members or case managers make a referral?

Yes. Family members, case managers, agencies and community organizations are welcome to reach out on behalf of a veteran through our contact or housing inquiry forms.
