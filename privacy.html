Privacy Policy

Phoenix Housing Group respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site (an intake form, a call request, a housing inquiry, a contact message or a newsletter signup), we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, phone number, email address and the details of your message.

How we use it

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, connect you with our intake team, evaluate housing and program requests, communicate with you about the program and, if you opt in, send occasional updates. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Sensitive information

You may choose to share information about your health, recovery or personal circumstances. We treat everything you share as confidential and use it only to help you. Please share only what you are comfortable sharing; nothing is required to start a conversation.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our team by a third-party form delivery service that processes your submission only to forward it to us.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to update or delete the information you have shared with us, or to stop receiving updates, by contacting us through the contact form.

Nondiscrimination and Fair Housing

Phoenix Housing Group is an Equal Housing Opportunity provider. We welcome everyone who is ready to rebuild, and we do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, national origin, age, sexual orientation or gender identity, in keeping with the Fair Housing Act and applicable Nevada law. We use respectful, person-first language in everything we write.

Accessibility

We are committed to making this website usable by everyone, and we aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. If you have trouble using any part of this site, or need information in another format, please reach out through the contact form and we will help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be posted on this page.
