Privacy Policy

Phoenix Housing Group respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information we collect

When you submit a form on this site (a contact message, housing inquiry or newsletter signup), we collect the information you choose to provide, such as your name, email address, phone number and the details of your message.

How we use it

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your inquiry, evaluate housing requests, communicate with you about our program and, if you opt in, send occasional updates. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Form processing

Form submissions are delivered to our team by a third-party form delivery service. That service processes your submission only for the purpose of forwarding it to us.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not use advertising cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to update or delete the information you have shared with us, or to stop receiving updates, by contacting us through the contact form.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The most current version will always be posted on this page.
